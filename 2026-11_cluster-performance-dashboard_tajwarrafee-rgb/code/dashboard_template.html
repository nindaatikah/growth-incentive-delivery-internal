Definitions and comparison rules

These rules apply to every city in the dropdown. Delivered orders, all delivery verticals, dated by the local order-creation date. History starts 1 January 2025. Orders outside that city’s clusters are excluded. Each order is assigned to a cluster from its customer delivery zone. Bubble positions are the average delivery coordinates of that cluster and do not move between periods.

MetricMeaning
Segment The cluster’s role in the November 2026 capital allocation, such as Maintain Share, Non Priority, Launch, Preparation, Monetise, or Grow Share. It stays the same in every period. Blank when that cluster has no segment on the sheet.
Active merchants Distinct providers with at least one delivered order in the cluster during the selected period. A merchant can serve more than one cluster, so the cluster counts do not add up to the city total.
GMV share The cluster’s GMV as a percentage of that city’s GMV across its clusters in the same period.
Order share The cluster’s delivered orders as a percentage of that city’s delivered orders across its clusters in the same period.
  • Weekly compares with the previous week and the same ISO week last year. Monthly compares with the previous month and the same month last year. The current incomplete week and month are excluded.
  • The yearly tab shows the full prior calendar year and the current year through the last complete week. Year-on-year for the current year uses the same dates last year, not the full prior year.
  • A percentage change is blank when the prior value is zero. Changes based on fewer than 30 orders in the prior period are marked unstable and drawn lighter on the map.
  • Bolt spend as a percentage of city GMV uses the city total across its clusters as the denominator. Spend / GMV uses the cluster’s own GMV.
  • Bubble area is scaled so small clusters stay visible. It ranks clusters by the selected volume metric; it is not a geographic area.
  • On the weekly tab, objective spend is campaign Bolt spend for orders delivered in the selected cluster. It can differ slightly from the Bolt spend card, which uses the order-level spend total.
